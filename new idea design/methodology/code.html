Methodology

Before we write a single line of code, we align on human impact.

We begin with a deep dive discovery phase, engaging stakeholders to define the core problem rather than just listing features. We map out user journeys, technical constraints, and business goals to create a robust, phased architecture plan.

Timelines vary based on complexity, but a typical engagement starts with a 3-4 week strategy and design phase, followed by 8-12 weeks of iterative development. We prioritize shipping a functional MVP early to gather real-world data.

We believe in conscious engineering. While speed to market is critical, we establish a scalable foundation from day one. We allocate specific cycles during the build process to refactor and optimize, ensuring long-term maintainability.
